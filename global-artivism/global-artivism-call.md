## Goal
Open a call inviting illustrators, field-notebook keepers, ecologists, farmers, and local people to make and share place-based art about the Côa Valley and rewilding, inside Global Artivism Month, as a locally rooted action listed in the movement's Mosaic.

## Steps
1. **Decide the ask.** Three levels, mirroring the movement's own: *Witness/amplify* (share posts), *Contribute* (one drawing/page from the field or from home), *Co-create* (collective map/zine of the Côa corridor). Pick one shared theme and one format (e.g. A5 page, "one place, one species, one sentence").
2. **Register with the movement.** Fill the Participation Form as an organiser; list the events/actions; check the T&Cs commitments and whether Sunrise Relay slots exist for Europe/Portugal.
3. **Check with coa-connectivity-lab.** Confirm they are happy with the framing, credit, and image use. Set a consent and licence line (e.g. CC BY-NC, artist keeps rights, opt-in to the printed zine).
4. **Write the call** (plain language, short): what, who, when, how to join, what you get (credit, a place in the zine, being listed in the Mosaic), what is not required (no experience, no travel). Add Portuguese and English versions; French optional.
5. **Build sign-up.** A simple form (name, place, level of involvement, medium, consent, contact). Pick a tool that fits an open-source preference (e.g. an open form, or a GitHub Discussion in `coa-connectivity-lab`, like Discussions #24/#25).
6. **Outreach channels.** WILDLABS (already posted there), GitHub org Discussion, LinkedIn, Rewilding Portugal channels, local Côa/Foz Côa contacts, urban sketchers/nature journaling groups, Global Artivism Europe contacts from the convening, and the earlier respondents to the movement's call.
7. **Onboard.** Welcome pack: theme, how to submit, calendar, safety and land-respect notes, and a template page. One short kickoff call ~26 Oct.
8. **Run the month.** Weekly prompt, weekly share round-up, and a submissions folder. Each week's post is tagged for the movement's daily editorial dispatch.
9. **Close.** Curate a gallery or zine, post on 10 Dec, write a short outcome note, and log it in `notes/`.

## Risks and open questions
- Terms & Conditions or naming rules may limit how the call can use the Global Artivism identity, so check before launch.
- Keep the scope small (one theme, one format, one form). The co-create level is optional.
- Soft-launch to coa-connectivity-lab first if needed.
- Language and access: offer low-tech ways to join (photo of a paper page by message).
- The "Starter" recipe book captured only 2 of 16 pages, so get the full text before promising features.
