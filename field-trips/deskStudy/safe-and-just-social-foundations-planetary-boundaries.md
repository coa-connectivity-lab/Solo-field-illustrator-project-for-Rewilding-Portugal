Ten social foundations and ten planetary boundaries: what "safe and just" actually measures
Linda Angulo Lopez, compiled 23 August 2026, desk research operationalizing the corrected "safe and just" framing from `safe-and-just-indigenous-science-earth-system-justice.md`

The companion note sets out who "safe and just" belongs to: Indigenous Science and community-led climate justice movements as the origin of the underlying idea, Joyeeta Gupta's Earth Commission work as the academic articulation this project draws its quantified language from. This note operationalizes that framing at a finer grain, into two concrete lists Linda pulled from an interactive planetary-boundaries reference tool: ten social foundations and ten planetary boundaries. Both lists are cited to the paper that started the whole planetary-boundaries lineage, Rockström et al. 2009, "[Planetary Boundaries: Exploring the Safe Operating Space for Humanity](https://www.ecologyandsociety.org/vol14/iss2/art32/)," *Ecology and Society* 14(2):32, though the specific ten-and-ten framing (rather than the original nine boundaries, twelve foundations) reflects a later, expanded operationalization of the same underlying model.

(i) [Food and Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_security)
(ii) [Health and Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health) / [Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education)
(iii) [Income, Work and AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Income) / [Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)
(iv) [Peace & Justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peace)
(v) [Political Voice and Networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy) / [Flow Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flow_network)
(vi) [Social and Gender Equity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_equity) / [Gender Equality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gender_equality)
(vii) [Climate Migration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_migrant)
(viii) [Capital in the 21st Century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_in_the_Twenty-First_Century)
(ix) [Energy Poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy_poverty)
(x) [Water Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_security)

## Ten planetary boundaries

(i) [Climate Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change)
(ii) [Ocean Acidification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocean_acidification)
(iii) [Chemical Pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planetary_boundaries#Chemical_pollution)
(iv) [Nitrogen & Phosphorus Loading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fertilizer#Environmental_effects)
(v) [Freshwater Withdrawals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overdrafting)
(vi) [Land Conversion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_use#Environment)
(vii) [Genetic Biodiversity Loss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity_loss)
(viii) [Biodiversity Loss by Disturbance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity_loss)
(ix) [Air Pollution and Ozone Depletion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution) / [Ozone Depletion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozone_depletion)
(x) [New Entities, such as Emerging Infectious Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emerging_infectious_disease)

This list operationalizes the *biophysical* half of "safe and just" at a finer grain than the rest of this project has used so far. It is not a competing model to Gupta's Earth system justice work, and it is not a return to crediting Raworth: it is the original Rockström-lineage boundary set, expanded into ten-and-ten form, sitting underneath the justice framing the companion note establishes.

## Climate change and biodiversity loss: the two boundaries this project's own work bears on

Two of the ten planetary boundaries above, climate change and biodiversity loss (both the "genetic" and "by disturbance" forms), are not abstract for this project. They show up directly in work already done and work this v5 revision adds:

**The Côa Valley itself.** The eco-connectivity notebook's own fire-barrier work (cells 18-21, MODIS MCD64A1 burned-area data) and its running annual-review narrative of 2025 and 2026 fires already document a landscape under active climate and biodiversity pressure, not a hypothetical one. That material is not repeated here; it is the local instance of exactly these two boundaries.

**Portugal's water resources.** The companion note `portugal-water-history-rivers-seas.md` documents drought and water-scarcity data for the Portuguese interior, the freshwater-withdrawals and climate-change boundaries made concrete at national scale.

**Brazil.** Amazon deforestation has fallen substantially in the most recent reporting: the Legal Amazon lost 5,796 km² between August 2024 and July 2025, down 11.08% and the lowest rate in 11 years, per Brazil's PRODES satellite-monitoring system, run by INPE, the National Institute for Space Research ([Secretaria de Comunicação Social, Brazilian government](https://www.gov.br/secom/en/latest-news/2025/10/in-2025-deforestation-fell-by-11-08-percent-in-the-amazon-and-by-11-49-percent-in-the-cerrado); [WWF Brasil](https://www.wwf.org.br/en/?93221%2FDeforestation-falls-by-11-in-Amazon-and-Cerrado-in-a-year=)). The more recent DETER alert system recorded 2,874 km² of deforestation alerts from August 2025 through July 2026, down 36% and the lowest since 2013 ([Mongabay](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/08/amazon-deforestation-alerts-fall-to-lowest-level-since-2013-brazilian-data-show/)). That improving trend sits alongside a real climate-change interaction worth naming honestly: the exceptional 2024 drought caused 2.78 million hectares of primary forest loss, roughly 60% of it from fire, and INPE's own data shows a rising share of Amazon loss now coming from fire-associated "progressive degradation" rather than clear-cutting, 38% of the most recent period's loss. Falling clear-cutting and rising fire-driven degradation are two different trends, not one improving story.

**Angola.** The Benguela Current Large Marine Ecosystem, the upwelling system this project's companion note `portugal-land-to-sea-ocean-currents-colonial-routes.md` documents as historically obstructing and now sustaining Angola's fisheries, is a documented global marine warming hotspot: climate change is both weakening and strengthening the upwelling cycle in different parts of the region and reducing nutrient supply, with direct implications for the commercial and artisanal fisheries thousands of people depend on ([Benguela Current Convention, climate change project](https://www.benguelacc.org/climate-change-project/); [Climate Dynamics: "Long-term climatological trends driving the recent warming along the Angolan and Namibian coasts"](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00382-024-07305-z)).

**Mozambique.** Coral reefs in the Quirimbas Archipelago, in northern Mozambique, sit at the edge of a coral-diversity climate-adaptive center, one with limited room to expand because of geologic and oceanographic limits to its south, according to a peer-reviewed study of environmental variability in the region. That same study found climate disturbances had a larger negative impact on coral benthic cover than human influences did, even near urban areas ([McClanahan, Ecosphere 2017, "Environmental variability indicates a climate-adaptive center under threat in northern Mozambique coral reefs"](https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ecs2.1812)). Bazaruto Island's reefs, further south, showed no substantial change after the major 1998 regional bleaching event and host 99 Scleractinia and 29 Alcyonacea coral species, a genuinely resilient result worth noting alongside the more vulnerable Quirimbas finding rather than collapsing the two into one regional story.

## What this section is not

This is not a claim that the Côa Valley, Portugal, Brazil, Mozambique, and Angola face the same climate and biodiversity pressures, or that this project has modelled any of these five contexts quantitatively beyond what its own eco-connectivity pipeline already does for the Côa Valley itself. It is a deliberate widening of the lens, using two of the ten planetary boundaries above as the through-line, before the notebook's closing sections on water, empire, and workforce develop each thread in full.
