## Habitat description

A paved single-track lane runs through open dehesa/montado-type countryside: scattered mature holm oak (*Quercus ilex*, cf. — the round-crowned, dark evergreen canopy matches, though leaf detail isn't resolvable at this distance) standing over dry, cured-gold grassland, with dry-stone field walls and wire fencing running alongside the road. One frame is a close granite boulder-and-scrub outcrop off the road — lichen-crusted rock, low broom/gorse-type shrub, and wind-stunted oak, the same rock-and-scrub terrain type documented elsewhere in the Côa Valley notes (Vale das Éguas, Amoreira). The grass is fully cured for the season (pale gold, no green), consistent with late-August drought stress across the whole route today.
