## Habitat description

The standard access route for this sector, descends by 4x4 track from the Castelo Melhor reception centre about 6 km into the Côa valley, past dry-stone walls, olive groves, and almond groves, with a panoramic view over the terraced vineyards below Quinta da Ervamoira, one of the historic Douro-style quintas on the Côa's banks (visible in `agricultural.jpeg`/`dba0f60d-e134-471a-828f-cff1fda16901.jpeg`, a duplicate of the same valley view, dry scrub and tall grass in the foreground, terraced vineyard and olive blocks on the slope, storm light over the ridgeline). The track ends at the river, from where it's a short walk to the first of the panel groups. This is schist terrain, the same fine-grained rock that carries the engravings themselves (`horse in movement-gravure.jpeg`, `gravure-fish.jpeg`), cut and weathered into sheet-like slabs.

## Page 3 — Connectivity barriers

| Barrier | Type | Permeability |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Côa Dam (Barragem de Foz Côa), planned but cancelled in 1996 specifically to protect these engravings, after a two-year civic and parliamentary movement | Mostly historic, but not fully resolved | The reason this stretch of the Côa is still a free-flowing, connected river corridor, and the reason the rock art and its landscape survive intact enough to visit today. Correction to log: this isn't purely historical — an abandoned cofferdam from the cancelled works was never demolished, and it still periodically backs up water across ~10 km of the valley, submerging panels for a documented 500+ days since 1996. Full history and sourcing in `deskStudy/movimento-salvaguarda-gravuras-foz-coa.md` |
| 2 | Dry-stone terracing and olive/almond/vineyard field boundaries along the descent track | Agricultural land-use edge | Partial barrier for small ground fauna moving between valley strata; more porous for birds and larger mammals following the track itself |
| 3 | Unpaved 4x4 access track, Castelo Melhor to the river (~6 km) | Vehicle track | Easily crossable for wildlife; the main constraint it imposes is on visitor access, not on animal movement |
