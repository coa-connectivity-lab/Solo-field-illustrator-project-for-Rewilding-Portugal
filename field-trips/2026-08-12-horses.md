# Fences horses

Location checks out only partly: the confirmed Sorraia sites are Vale Carapito (Vilar Maior, Sabugal) and Ermo das Águias (Vale de Madeira, Pinhel), and Lageosa isn't listed as one of them in anything I can find from Rewilding Portugal's own reporting. That said, the phenotype in your photos, pale grey/dun body with black mane, tail, and legs (black points), matches the Sorraia description closely, mouse/bay dun coat with mule stripe and zebra-type leg markings. I can't confirm the dorsal stripe from these angles.

Honest answer: **plausible but not confirmed**. Either this is an unreported expansion of the herd beyond the two named sites, or these are locally free-ranging horses that happen to carry similar primitive colouring, which isn't unusual in this region regardless of the official reintroduction programme. Worth checking for an ear tag, brand, or nearby signage next time, that would settle it on the spot.
