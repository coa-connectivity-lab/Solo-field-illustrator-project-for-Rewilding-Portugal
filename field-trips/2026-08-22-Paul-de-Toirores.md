## Habitat description

Two wide shots show the lagoon/wetland itself: dark, still water lined with dense willow-type shrub and reed, set in a shallow bowl of dry cured-gold grassland and scrub, with woodland forming the horizon line beyond. Reeds and low aquatic vegetation grow right at the waterline in the more sheltered inlets. A third, closer frame shows the same water body from a different angle, with a village roofline (white walls, red tile) visible on the skyline beyond the reeds — placing the wetland close to a settlement edge rather than deep in open country. This matches the site's role as a restored wetland reconnecting habitat close to human land use, the framing already established for the Water theme.

The open grassland surrounding the water is fully cured (pale gold, no green), same late-August drought condition as the rest of today's route.
