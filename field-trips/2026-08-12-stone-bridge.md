## Habitat description

A small stone footbridge crossing a slow, shallow headwater stream just outside Fóios, bordered by dry stone walls, orchard trees, and rough pasture on one side, and a denser bramble/willow thicket on the other. The streambed is shallow and rocky with patches of low aquatic vegetation at the margins, this is upstream, headwater character consistent with being close to the Côa's source.
