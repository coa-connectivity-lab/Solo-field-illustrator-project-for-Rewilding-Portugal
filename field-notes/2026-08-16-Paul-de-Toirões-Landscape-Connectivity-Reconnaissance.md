**Date:** 16 August 2026

**Location:**  
Paul de Toirões, Portugal

**Starting point:**  
Residencial Morgado, Almeida

**Travel:**
- Distance: approximately 30.5 km
- Driving time: approximately 30 minutes each way
- Route: Almeida → N332 → Paul de Toirões

---

## Purpose

A reconnaissance day focused on understanding Paul de Toirões within its wider landscape system.

The objective is to study how ecological connectivity is shaped by the relationship between:

- restored habitats
- agricultural land
- settlements
- roads
- infrastructure
- historical land use

This provides the wider context for the **Water** theme:

**Water Returns, Life Returns**
