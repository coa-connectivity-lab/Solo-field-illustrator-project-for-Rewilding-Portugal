# Full-Day Field Exercise — Ribeira do Mosteiro Focused Landscape Study

**Date:** 25 August 2026

**Theme:**
**Connection — Landscapes Linking Habitats, Species, and Human History**

**Location:**
Ribeira do Mosteiro, Sernancelhe, Portugal

**Purpose:**

A focused illustration development day centred on the stream corridor, valley structure, cliffs, Mediterranean vegetation, and habitat connections identified during the previous reconnaissance.

The objective is to transform landscape observations into visual material for the **Connection** illustration plate.

---

# Morning — Stream Corridor and Valley Study

Focus on the relationship between water, geology, and vegetation.

Observe:

## Stream System

Record:

* stream structure
* water movement
* riparian vegetation
* pools, channels, and edges
* connections between wet and dry habitats

Sketch:

* stream corridor compositions
* reflections and water patterns
* vegetation along water margins
* relationships between foreground and distant valley structures
