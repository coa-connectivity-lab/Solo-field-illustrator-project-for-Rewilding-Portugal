6350-210 Almeida, Portugal

**Travel:**

* Distance: approximately 40.9 km

* Route: Almeida → N340 → N324 → Estr. do Côa → Vale Carapito

---

## Purpose

A reconnaissance day focused on understanding Vale Carapito within its wider landscape system.

The objective is to study how ecological connectivity is shaped by the relationship between:

* woodland habitats
* open landscapes
* agricultural areas
* meadow systems
* terrain structure
* water features
* human land-use patterns

This visit provides the landscape-scale context for the **Movement** theme:

**Movement — Animals, Land, and Seasonal Change**

The purpose of this day is to understand the structure and function of the landscape before the focused illustration development session.
