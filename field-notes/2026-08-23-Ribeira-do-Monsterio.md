# Ribeira do Mosteiro, documentation summary

**Location and scale:** north bank of the Douro River, municipality of Freixo-de-Espada-à-Cinta, inside the Douro International Natural Park. Rewilding Portugal frames it explicitly as a landscape-connectivity site, this matches your Connection theme directly rather than needing reinterpretation.

**History and habitat:** rugged valley with distinctive geological formations, old olive and almond groves now recolonising with holm oak, juniper, and terebinth as the rewilding process advances, the original Mediterranean forest composition reasserting itself. The Mosteiro Stream at the valley floor maintains enough humidity to support a notably rich flora against the surrounding dry terrain. The cliffs are described as a refuge for endangered bird species specifically because of their height and inaccessibility.

**Cultural layer:** the Calçada de Alpajares is a medieval paved path, possibly dating to the first Portuguese dynasty, historically the main route linking the Miranda lands in the north to the Ribacôa region in the south, in use until early 20th-century road building superseded it. At the route's highest point, an old dovecote and medieval rock-cut graves remain visible, worth noting for the same "human history plus rewilding" framing you're already using at other sites.
