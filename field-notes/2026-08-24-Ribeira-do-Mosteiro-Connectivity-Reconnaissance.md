## Purpose

A reconnaissance day focused on understanding Ribeira do Mosteiro as a connected landscape system within the wider region.

The objective is to document how geological structure, water systems, vegetation communities, and human land use interact to create ecological connections across the valley.

This visit establishes the landscape context for the **Connection** theme:

**Connection — Landscapes Linking Habitats, Species, and Human History**

The focus is on understanding the valley as a functioning ecological network before developing the final illustration composition.
