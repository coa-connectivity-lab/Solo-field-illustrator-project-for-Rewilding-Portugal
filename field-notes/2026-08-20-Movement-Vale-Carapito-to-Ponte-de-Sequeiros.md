## Purpose

A focused field study to develop the **Movement** illustration theme through direct observation of landscape dynamics.

The objective is to understand how animal movement, grazing systems, vegetation structure, and landscape form interact within the Greater Côa Valley.

Primary study elements:

* Sorraia horse observations (if visible)
* grazing dynamics
* woodland/meadow relationships
* animal pathways and habitat use
* development of the main illustration composition

This session builds directly on the landscape connectivity reconnaissance completed on 19 August.
