# Post-Trip Development Plan

Following the field residency, the project will move into a structured consolidation and development phase. The objective is to transform field observations, sketches, photographs, and ecological notes into a coherent illustration and landscape analysis framework.

During the residency itself, field and consolidation days now alternate rather than following a fixed calendar, so the observations arriving in this phase are already partly organised by the time it starts.

The post-trip workflow will focus on:

* reconciling field observations across all study locations
* organising visual references and field documentation
* refining the three illustration narratives
* analysing ecological connections between landscapes, including the Douro/Côa comparison from Porto
* developing the final Story Map presentation

## Final Development Outcome

The post-trip phase will integrate field research and illustration practice into a single narrative structure showing how landscapes function as interconnected systems.

The final Story Map will communicate:

* the Greater Côa Valley landscape context
* the role of Almeida as an observation base
* ecological relationships across the three principal study sites, Vale Carapito, Paul de Toirões, and Ribeira do Mosteiro
* the themes of **Movement**, **Water**, and **Connection**
* the Douro as a point of contrast, a heavily dammed river against the Côa's history of resisting damming
* the relationship between natural history, landscape processes, and human interaction with place
