## Full-Day Field Exercise — Merujes do Paulo e André Reconnaissance

**Date:** 15 August 2026

**Location:**  
Merujes do Paulo e André, Naves, Portugal  
J4XC+27, Naves, Portugal

**Starting point:**  
Residencial Morgado, Almeida

**Travel:**
- Driving distance: approximately 17 km
- Driving time: approximately 30 minutes each way

**Purpose:**

A full-day reconnaissance mission to evaluate Merujes do Paulo e André as a supplementary landscape study site within the Greater Côa Valley Natural History Illustration project.

The objective is to build visual understanding of the landscape before deciding whether it contributes to the final illustration narratives.
