I would like to valorise the work I have done so far, in eco-connectivity in particular and on rights of water alongside it, and I am prospecting activities with the Society for Earth Law on the second of these.

The piece I have drafted compares South Africa's statutory Reserve, the legally binding minimum allocation for basic human needs and ecosystem health under the National Water Act 1998, with the absence of any equivalent floor in Portuguese or EU law. The concrete Côa application is the stalled protocol to remove the Foz Côa cofferdam, signed in 2023 and still awaiting approval, on a river system that already crosses into Spain.

(i) the pipeline paper itself, framed as a reproducible template for resource-constrained conservation organisations rather than a methodological advance over Circuitscape;
