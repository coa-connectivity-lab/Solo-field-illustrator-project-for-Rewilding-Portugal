# #Field trip plan](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wNyYlElJhagsg_7NNhoL7V2yz-BWiEKrkB091rVS1sM/edit?usp=sharing), Greater Côa Valley residence
Linda Angulo Lopez, updated 17 August 2026

## Strategy

Two phases, built around real constraints: Software Heritage remote work obligations
(especially 11-19 Aug), an Almeida accommodation base, travel time between sites, hot
summer conditions, limited daylight comfort, and Paul de Toirões requiring a guide.

### Phase 1, 11-19 August, exploration and reference collection
Goal: collect material, not finish artwork. Low-pressure observation only.
Field rhythm: 2-4 hour excursions, early morning or late afternoon, photography and
reference collection, quick sketches, field notes. No long midday walks, no unrealistic
equipment loads, no attempting finished drawings in the field.

### Phase 2, 20-27 August, focused field residency
Goal: return to priority locations and produce the main drawings, plus the Porto/Douro
comparison trip.

### Site priority
(i) Vale Carapito, highest, clear ecological story, repeat visits possible, strong
    central subject.
(ii) Paul de Toirões, high, unique restoration story, but access-dependent on a guide.
(iii) Ribeira do Mosteiro, medium, spectacular but visually complex and harder to
    complete.

### Progress so far

(i) Río Côa, mouth to Almeida, explored.
(ii) Prehistoric rock engravings, visited.

#### Realistic end-of-trip outcome

Finished or near-finished: 3 landscape compositions, 9-12 supporting sketches, field
notebook, species observations, photographic/reference archive.

Not realistic during the trip: three museum-quality finished plates, complete species
inventories, exhaustive ecological diagrams. These develop after returning home, in the
post-trip phase (W38 field reconciliation, W39 illustration and connectivity analysis,
W42 final Story Map development).
