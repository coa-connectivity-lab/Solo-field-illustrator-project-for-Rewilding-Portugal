                    coa-connectivity-lab-survey
                    **Purpose:** A single flexible Survey123 form for field data collection in the Greater Côa Valley, covering both species observations and physical barriers to wildlife movement, designed to feed directly into the existing coa-connectivity-lab connectivity pipeline rather than sit as a separate dataset.

**Structure:**
- **Visit header**: date/time, site name, weather/light conditions, GPS location.
- **Species observation** (repeatable): functional group (constrained to your existing categories: large herbivores, decomposers, apex predators, mesocarnivores, riverine fish, invasive species), species/common name, photo, count/abundance, behaviour notes, habitat description, restoration-evidence note.
- **Connectivity barrier** (repeatable):  barrier type (road, motorway, fence, dam, weir, culvert, urban edge, agricultural boundary, other), photo, permeability assessment (blocking/partial/crossable/unknown), notes on how it affects movement.

**Scope covered:** started with horses and dung beetles at Vale Carapito, then expanded to also include wildcat, native fish, and invasive species — plus the barrier-tracking piece, which ground-truths the resistance-surface assumptions already baked into your `base_layers` rasters (roads, motorway, ruggedness, slope).

**Still open:**
- The exact species list for a constrained dropdown (you'd need to confirm the scientific name for the fish species you called "native rabbit fish").
- Manual setup in Survey123 web designer after the AI draft: geopoint field, the two repeat groups, and locking the functional-group field to your exact registry categories — none of these the assistant generates on its own.
