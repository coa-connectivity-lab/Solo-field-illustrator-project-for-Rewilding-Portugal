# Greater Côa Valley Natural History Illustration Strategy — August 2026

**[Solo field illustrator project for Rewilding Portugal*](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wNyYlElJhagsg_7NNhoL7V2yz-BWiEKrkB091rVS1sM/edit?usp=sharing)*

## Project Overview

This repository contains the planning, field studies, sketches, ecological notes, and illustration development for a natural history illustration project in the Greater Côa Valley, Portugal.

The project explores how rewilding restores ecological processes through three connected landscape stories:

1. **Movement** — herbivores restoring ecological dynamics  
2. **Water** — wetlands recovering after human disturbance  
3. **Connection** — landscapes allowing ecosystems to function together  

The final outcome will be a triptych of field-study landscape plates supported by species sketches, habitat studies, and ecological observations.

---

# Project Objectives

The goal is not to complete three museum-quality finished plates during the field trip.

The objective is to produce:

- 3 strong landscape compositions
- 9–12 supporting organism/habitat sketches
- field notebooks
- ecological observations
- photographic reference archive
- preliminary illustration studies for later refinement

# Final Triptych Concept

## 1. Vale Carapito — Movement

### Working title
**A Landscape Returning to Life**

### Ecological story

Large herbivores reconnect ecological processes by influencing:

- vegetation structure
- nutrient cycles
- habitat diversity
- landscape dynamics

### Main plate

Landscape composition showing:

- valley structure
- meadow
- woodland
- river corridor
- traces of agricultural history
- Sorraia horses (if visible)

### Supporting studies

- Sorraia horse behaviour and anatomy
- dry stone walls and agricultural heritage
- oak woodland regeneration

---

## 2. Paul de Toirões — Water

### Working title
**Water Returns, Life Returns**

### Ecological story

A modified landscape becomes a refuge where restored water systems support biodiversity.

### Main plate

Landscape composition showing:

- lagoon
- aquatic vegetation
- forest edge
- wetland habitat

### Supporting studies

- black stork behaviour
- aquatic vegetation
- mining landscape transformation

### Access note

Guided access required.

Priority visit during the August field period.

---

## 3. Ribeira do Mosteiro — Connection

### Working title
**A Valley Connecting Life**

### Ecological story

A river valley acts as an ecological bridge between habitats.

### Main plate

Landscape composition showing:

- cliffs
- valley structure
- stream
- Mediterranean vegetation

### Supporting studies

- griffon vulture silhouette
- schist geology
- holm oak/juniper vegetation

# Project Principles

## 1. Observe before drawing

The landscape story comes first.

## 2. One strong image is better than many incomplete ideas

Each plate needs a clear ecological message.

## 3. Record processes, not just species

The focus is:

- movement
- water
- connection

## 4. Leave room for refinement

The field trip creates the foundation. Studio development continues afterwards.
